Georgia | a land of mountains, ancient villages and wild landscapes

Georgia is a breathtaking country in the Caucasus, located between Europe and Asia, where snow-covered mountains, deep valleys, lush forests and the shores of the Black Sea create an extraordinary variety of landscapes. Bordering Russia, Turkey, Armenia and Azerbaijan, it is particularly famous for the Greater Caucasus mountain range, traditional stone villages and its rich cultural heritage.

Among its most impressive sights are Kazbegi – a dramatic mountain region dominated by the 5,054-metre Mount Kazbek; Svaneti – a remote alpine region with medieval stone towers and enormous glaciers; and Martvili Canyon, with its emerald-coloured waters and forest-covered cliffs. Further east, the colourful hills of Kakheti and the wine-growing valleys offer a completely different landscape, while the Black Sea coast around Batumi provides subtropical vegetation and sandy beaches.

Itinerary

Day 1 – Arrival in Tbilisi

Arrive at Tbilisi International Airport and transfer to your hotel in the city centre. Spend the afternoon exploring the Old Town, with its narrow streets, traditional balconies and historic churches. Walk up to Narikala Fortress for panoramic views over the city and the Kura River. Overnight in a hotel in Tbilisi.

Day 2 – Exploring Tbilisi and Mtskheta

Spend the morning discovering more of Tbilisi before travelling approximately 30 minutes to Mtskheta, Georgia's ancient capital. Visit Jvari Monastery, situated on a hill overlooking the meeting point of the Aragvi and Mtkvari rivers, and explore the historic Svetitskhoveli Cathedral. Return to Tbilisi for the night.

Day 3 – The Caucasus Mountains and Kazbegi

Leave Tbilisi and travel north along the Georgian Military Highway towards Stepantsminda, also known as Kazbegi. Stop at Ananuri Fortress, overlooking the turquoise Zhinvali Reservoir, and continue through the dramatic mountain scenery of Gudauri and Jvari Pass. Arrive in Stepantsminda, a small mountain town surrounded by enormous peaks. Overnight in a mountain guesthouse.

Day 4 – Mount Kazbek and Gergeti Trinity Church

Spend the day exploring the spectacular landscapes around Kazbegi. Visit Gergeti Trinity Church, perched on a mountainside at approximately 2,170 metres, with impressive views of Mount Kazbek rising behind it. Continue towards Gveleti Waterfalls, where short walking trails lead through a dramatic mountain valley. Return to Stepantsminda for another night.

Day 5 – Journey west to Kutaisi

Begin the long drive west towards Kutaisi, crossing the Caucasus foothills and travelling through central Georgia. Stop along the way to enjoy the changing landscapes, from high mountain scenery to greener valleys and forests. Arrive in Kutaisi in the afternoon or evening and settle into a local guesthouse or hotel.

Day 6 – Martvili Canyon and Prometheus Cave

Explore two of western Georgia's most remarkable natural attractions. Start at Martvili Canyon, famous for its emerald-green river, steep limestone walls and waterfalls surrounded by dense vegetation. Continue to Prometheus Cave, an enormous underground cave system filled with impressive stalactites, stalagmites and illuminated chambers. Return to Kutaisi for the night.

Day 7 – Journey to Svaneti and Mestia

Travel north towards the remote mountain region of Svaneti, following the Enguri River through deep valleys and increasingly dramatic mountain scenery. Stop near the impressive Enguri Dam before continuing along winding mountain roads to Mestia. This traditional mountain town is famous for its medieval stone defensive towers and spectacular views of the surrounding Caucasus peaks. Overnight in a guesthouse in Mestia.

Day 8 – Ushguli and the highest mountain villages

Take a day trip to Ushguli, a collection of traditional mountain villages situated at approximately 2,100 metres above sea level. Surrounded by enormous peaks and overlooked by Mount Shkhara, Georgia's highest mountain at 5,203 metres, the villages are famous for their medieval stone towers and remote location. Explore the village and enjoy the alpine scenery before returning to Mestia. Overnight in Mestia.

Day 9 – From the mountains to the Black Sea

Leave Mestia and travel southwest towards Batumi, watching the landscape gradually change from rugged mountain valleys to greener lowlands and subtropical vegetation. Arrive on the Black Sea coast and spend the evening exploring Batumi's seaside promenade, botanical surroundings and modern waterfront. Overnight in a hotel in Batumi.

Day 10 – Batumi and departure

Spend the morning enjoying the Black Sea coastline or visiting the Batumi Botanical Garden, known for its extensive collection of plants from different parts of the world. Afterwards, transfer to Batumi International Airport for your return journey. Alternatively, travellers can take a train to Tbilisi if their international flight departs from there.
