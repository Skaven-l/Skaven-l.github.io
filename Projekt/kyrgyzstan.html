Kyrgyzstan | a land of alpine lakes and nomadic wilderness

Kyrgyzstan is a spectacular country in Central Asia, where enormous mountain ranges, turquoise alpine lakes, vast grasslands and remote valleys create some of the region's most extraordinary landscapes. Dominated by the Tian Shan mountains, with peaks rising above 7,000 metres, much of the country consists of high-altitude wilderness, where horses roam freely and traditional nomadic lifestyles remain an important part of local culture.

Among its most impressive sights are Issyk-Kul – one of the world's largest alpine lakes, surrounded by snow-covered mountains; Song-Kul – a remote high-altitude lake at 3,016 metres, where traditional yurts and grazing horses fill the summer pastures; and Ala-Archa National Park, with its glaciers, steep valleys and towering peaks. Jeti-Oguz is famous for its enormous red sandstone formations, while Skazka Canyon stands out for its colourful, unusually shaped rock formations.

Itinerary

Day 1 – Arrival in Bishkek

Arrive at Manas International Airport and transfer to your hotel in Bishkek. Spend the afternoon exploring the capital, visiting Ala-Too Square and Osh Bazaar, where traditional food, spices and local handicrafts fill the market stalls. Enjoy your first introduction to Kyrgyz culture and cuisine. Overnight in a hotel in Bishkek.

Day 2 – Ala-Archa National Park

Travel approximately 45 minutes south of Bishkek to Ala-Archa National Park, a spectacular mountain valley surrounded by glaciers, forests and towering peaks. Explore the hiking trails along the Ala-Archa River, enjoy views of the surrounding mountains and discover the park's waterfalls and alpine scenery. Return to Bishkek for the night.

Day 3 – Burana Tower and Issyk-Kul

Leave Bishkek and travel east towards Issyk-Kul, stopping at Burana Tower, an ancient Silk Road minaret surrounded by archaeological remains and stone sculptures. Continue towards Cholpon-Ata, a lakeside town overlooking the enormous Issyk-Kul Lake. Spend the afternoon exploring the shoreline and enjoying views of the surrounding mountains. Overnight in a guesthouse near the lake.

Day 4 – Journey to Karakol

Follow the northern shore of Issyk-Kul towards Karakol, stopping at scenic viewpoints along the way. Once there, explore the town's colourful Dungan Mosque and traditional wooden Russian Orthodox cathedral. Visit the local market and discover the region's mixture of Kyrgyz, Dungan and Russian cultural influences. Overnight in a guesthouse in Karakol.

Day 5 – Jeti-Oguz Gorge and the Seven Bulls

Travel south of Karakol to Jeti-Oguz, famous for its enormous red sandstone cliffs known as the Seven Bulls. Explore the valley, where green forests and mountain rivers contrast with the striking red rock formations. Visit the Broken Heart rock formation and enjoy the surrounding alpine scenery before returning to Karakol. Overnight in Karakol.

Day 6 – Skazka Canyon and southern Issyk-Kul

Travel along the southern shore of Issyk-Kul, where the landscape becomes quieter and more remote. Stop at Skazka Canyon, also known as Fairy Tale Canyon, famous for its unusual red, orange and yellow rock formations shaped by wind and erosion. Continue towards Bokonbaevo, a traditional village known for its nomadic heritage and eagle-hunting traditions. Overnight in a local guesthouse.

Day 7 – Bokonbaevo and Kochkor

Spend the morning discovering the countryside around Bokonbaevo and learning about traditional Kyrgyz nomadic culture. Continue towards Kochkor, a village known for its traditional felt-making and handmade carpets called shyrdaks. Visit a local craft workshop to see how these colourful patterns are created. Overnight in a guesthouse in Kochkor.

Day 8 – Journey to Song-Kul Lake

Leave Kochkor in a 4WD vehicle and travel towards Song-Kul, crossing mountain passes and vast summer pastures known as jailoo. At approximately 3,016 metres above sea level, Song-Kul is surrounded by enormous grasslands where horses, sheep and cattle graze freely. Arrive at a traditional yurt camp, enjoy a local meal and experience the peaceful atmosphere of the high mountains. Overnight in a yurt.

Day 9 – Song-Kul and return to Bishkek

Wake up to spectacular views of the lake and surrounding mountains. Spend the morning exploring the area on foot, observing the traditional summer lifestyle of local herding families and enjoying the open landscapes. Afterwards, travel back towards Bishkek by 4WD, passing through mountain valleys and the scenic Boom Gorge. Overnight in a hotel in Bishkek.

Day 10 – Departure

Spend the morning enjoying a final walk through Bishkek or visiting a local market before transferring to Manas International Airport. Catch your return flight, usually connecting through a larger international hub such as Istanbul.
